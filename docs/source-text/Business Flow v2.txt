                                   Business Flow Diagrams

This document provides Business Flow Diagrams for the DEZOLVER platform, illustrating the end-to-end workflow followed by
each stakeholder. It explains how the Super Admin, Finance Team, HOD, Manager, and Student interact with the platform and
how their activities flow from one stage to another. The document gives new developers a clear understanding of the overall
business workflow, stakeholder responsibilities, and interactions before they begin working on the system.
